# RAG verification rules

Verification is independent and fail-closed. Require schema-valid evidence for every selected phase,
zero cross-tenant/source-ACL leakage, valid accessible citations, correct abstention, deletion and
reindex behavior, prompt-injection resistance, provider degradation, and PRD quality/latency/cost
thresholds. Compare approximate retrieval to an exact-search baseline. Unit-test counts or subjective
demo quality cannot substitute for the versioned evaluation set.
